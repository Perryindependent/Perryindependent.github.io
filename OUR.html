Perry Independent BAPTIST CHURCH

WE ARE AN INDEPENDENT BAPTIST CHURCH

We are not affiliated with any denomination or organization.We are a self-governing church and not controlled by a religious hierarchy.

We are a bible believing church we make no apology for saying that the bible is our sole authority and guide.we teach the word of God as it is to people as they are.

WE ARE FAMILY ORIENTED

We have Sunday School classes for all age groups and three adult classes to meet the need of every family.We also have many TEEN ACTIVITIES.

WE ARE MISSION MINDED

Missions

We at Perry Independent Baptist Church are greatly involved in missions. We have three full time missionaries sent out of our church and many others that we support. There are several ways in which we help our missionaries...

Faith Promise

We have chosen Faith Promise as the means in which we support our God-called missionaries. Our members give to Faith Promise (above their tithes and offerings) as the Lord directs them. This money is used solely for the purpose of monthly support and for the special needs of our missionaries.

Annual Missions Conference

During our Annual Missions Conference we prayerfully look for God's guidance in taking on new missionaries and increasing our personal Faith Promise support. It is also a time of being fed spiritually and learning more about missionaries, their calling and needs, and life on the mission field.

As commanded by our Lord we are striving to proclaim the gospel around the world as well as in our own community.
